## Notes

- **Verse D line 2** was rewritten to rhyme with line 1 (`fail` → `prevail`); the original liked
  fragment "Singing loud as we are able" was 8 syllables and did not rhyme with "fail".
- **Verse C line 6** filled with the liked phrase "Shout it loud and clear" (5 syll).
- Spare liked 5-syllable closers still available for other sections:
  "Sing it out again", "Best place you can be", "Having so much fun", "Brighten up your day".
- Line 3 ("Camp Bounce Twenty-Six") is fixed in every verse.
